The Four Template Pillars

Click these components in the sequence they must appear within a finalized standard source listing entry.

[03]

Author

Primary creator name listed as Last Name, First Name. Always conclude this initial element block with a period.

[01]

Title of Source

The specific name of the text. Italicize self-contained book titles, or place standalone essay titles within quotation marks.

[04]

Publisher

The enterprise responsible for distributing the final manuscript production. Omit standard corporate markers like LLC.

[02]

Publication Date

The calendar year or day variant designating release. Use international format styles for tracking specific days (e.g., 12 May 2026).
