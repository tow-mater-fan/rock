The Four Core Values

These pillars guide independent study habits, lab work, and exam parameters across all elective and core courses.

[03]

Honesty

Submitting work reflecting your true capabilities. Avoiding plagiarism by learning proper paraphrasing standards.

[01]

Fairness

Upholding peer standards during independent tasks. Completing examinations without external assistance networks.

[04]

Responsibility

Submitting original compositions. Reaching out to academic advisors or peer tutoring circles early when struggling.

[02]

Respect

Acknowledging intellectual contributions of authors, creators, and data platforms in every research draft.
